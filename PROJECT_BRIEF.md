# Project Brief — BP Electrical Website

## Client

**BP Electroinfra Pvt. Ltd.** (customer-facing brand: **BP Electrical**)

## Objective

Deliver a premium B2B corporate website with exactly ten primary pages, positioning the company as:

- Electrical product supplier
- Corporate electrical procurement partner
- Industrial electrical equipment supplier
- Electrical infrastructure contractor
- Complete electrical works service provider

## Audience

Procurement teams, project managers, EPC contractors, utility-linked organisations, industrial plant teams, and corporate facilities.

## Constraints

- No invented certifications, client approvals, project statistics, or technical specifications
- Utility names (e.g. Adani Electricity, Tata Power) referenced only as **working relationships**, not endorsements
- Project portfolio uses **marked placeholders** until verified content is supplied

## Deliverables

- Next.js application with responsive navigation, enquiry forms, WhatsApp links (when configured), SEO metadata, sitemap, robots.txt, and Organization structured data
- Brand-aligned design using uploaded logo colours, Manrope + Inter typography, and industrial photography
- Supporting documentation (design system, content map, SEO, components, assets)

## Success criteria

- All ten routes live and linked from header/footer
- Consistent header/footer, mobile navigation, accessible forms
- Production-ready build with environment-based contact details
