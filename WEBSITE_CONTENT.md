# Website Content Map

## Global messaging

**Legal entity:** BP Electroinfra Pvt. Ltd.  
**Brand:** BP Electrical  
**Tagline:** Electrical products, procurement and contracting for corporate and industrial clients.

## Page summaries

### Home

Hero, introduction, ecosystem associations, product categories, services, industries teaser, project placeholders, contact CTA.

### About Us

Company profile, capabilities list, mission, vision, approach.

### Products

Category overview, catalogue structure (no SKU-level specs), product enquiry form.

### Electrical Distribution Equipment

Switchgear, panels, cables, metering — category copy only; BOM enquiry.

### Industrial Electrical Products

Industrial line list; enquiry form.

### Electrical Contracting

Scope areas: complete works, installations, infrastructure, execution; tender enquiry.

### Electrical Supply & Procurement

Procurement pillars; RFQ-style enquiry.

### Projects & Work Experience

Explicit placeholders — no fabricated case studies.

### Industries We Serve

Six sector cards with short descriptions.

### Contact Us

`ContactDetails` + enquiry form; verified coordinates via env vars.

## Copy rules

- B2B tone; no lorem ipsum
- No fake testimonials or metrics
- Product pages describe **categories**, not unverified model numbers
- Partnership language: “working relationships” / “engagement with organisations such as…”

## Placeholder replacement checklist

1. `.env.local` contact fields
2. Project cards on `/projects` and Home
3. `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` for canonical URLs and JSON-LD
